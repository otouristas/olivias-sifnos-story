# Villa Olivia Clara - Luxury Villa Rental Website

## 🏖️ **Project Overview**

Villa Olivia Clara is a premium luxury villa rental website located in Sifnos, Greece. This modern, responsive website showcases a stunning 250m² villa with private pool, sea views, and authentic Greek hospitality. Built with cutting-edge SEO optimization and powered by advanced AI technology.

**Live Website**: https://villaoliviaclara.com

---

## ✨ **Key Features**

### **🏠 Villa Showcase**
- **4 Bedrooms, 4.5 Bathrooms** - Accommodates up to 8 guests
- **Private Pool** - 12m x 5m with 3m depth, cabana & bar area
- **Stunning Sea Views** - Panoramic views of Platis Gialos
- **Luxury Amenities** - Full kitchen, garden, BBQ, high-speed WiFi
- **Prime Location** - 7 minutes walk to Platis Gialos beach

### **🤖 AI-Powered Assistant**
